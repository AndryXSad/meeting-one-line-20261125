A program that writes the single sentence that could have replaced an entire pointless meeting to `/output/entry.txt`, for make.fun's "The Meeting That Should Have Been One Line" bounty. The line was picked by a local pairwise tournament over 20 original candidates, scored on the judge's five criteria in order: one sentence of office humor that kills the meeting, kills it outright, relatable to anyone who has sat through a useless one, lands as a joke with a sting, and stands alone. The winner (5-0 in the head-to-head round-robin of the top six):

> Approved, emailed, filed — and the meeting is still going because nobody has figured out how to end a calendar invite with dignity.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
cat out/entry.txt
```
